Geometry Plane and Arc Representation Grammar

A physical cross-section is incomplete without its plane, and a local circle radius is not the tokamak cylindrical radial coordinate merely because both DD leaves are named r. ISN must make those meanings grammatically distinct and round-trippable.

§ Decisions

Where must section-plane semantics be available?

A cross-section is incomplete unless the plane is part of the identity. The rule applies to hardware and any other carrier for which the section plane changes the physical object represented.

Locked by the lead as 1A: ISN must add the grammar segment, ordering, and vocabulary. The segment is generic; carrier-specific admissibility remains grammar/semantic validation.

May sample order or endpoint position enter the identity?

DD paths may contain first, second, start, or end positions, but those are sampling/provenance details.

Strict project rule: first, second, start, end, and equivalent ordered sample labels remain in DD provenance and never enter the Standard Name.

Objective
Extend the canonical ISN grammar so cross-sections name their plane and circle-arc geometry can express local representation quantities without being confused with global tokamak cylindrical coordinates. Preserve the strict rule that ordinal sample positions never enter identity.

Required work
1. Design and add a closed section_plane grammar segment with canonical ordering. Seed poloidal and add only other planes justified by the existing catalog/domain corpus.
2. Add or refine closed vocabulary and grammar structure for source-authoritative arc representation: local circle radius/centre/arc carrier semantics must be distinct from radial_coordinate in the tokamak cylindrical frame and from the physical outline of a conductor.
3. Define valid carrier/representation/plane combinations and reject missing or contradictory plane semantics where a cross-section requires a plane.
4. Keep start/end/first/second/third tokens out of every grammar segment. Ordered points remain source provenance only.
5. Update parser, composer, renderer, semantic checks, generated grammar maps, JSON schema/public grammar context, examples, and catalog guidance through the public API only.
6. Add round-trip and ambiguity tests using representative identities: poloidal cross-section of a conductor, a local circle radius for an arc representation, a global radial coordinate of a physical point, an outline coordinate, and a normal-vector component.
7. Cut and validate an ISN release candidate, then provide the exact dependency/version contract to imas-codex. Do not edit DD-source mappings or the Neo4j graph here.

Done when
- The new segment ordering and vocabulary round-trip through public parse/compose APIs.
- Missing/incorrect plane and global-vs-local radius confusions fail deterministically.
- No ordinal token is accepted into identity.
- imas-codex can consume the public grammar context without duplicating tokens or segment names.
