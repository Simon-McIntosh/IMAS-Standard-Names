§3 — The coordinator's read of concepts.yml, and its three corrections

The lead delegated the read of conventions/concepts.yml to the coordinator on 2026-10-14. All 46 entries were read. They are correct and consistent with the catalog's convention, and none names a COCOS. Three corrections landed.

  • Plasma boundary (node cnr-plasma-boundary-definition, commit f9965fd, merge 5bc8735, review 101/120). The locus definition in locus_registry.yml now reads, in the lead's words: The last closed magnetic flux surface: the outermost flux surface on which field lines close on themselves, bounding the confined plasma. The accessor returns it beside the equation ψ = ψLCFS. Gate: 39 passed at base and head.
  • Outboard and inboard midplane (node cnr-midplane-and-inductance, commit 0e43cde, review 102/120). Both were defined by dR/dZ = 0 on the separatrix, which is undefined for a limited plasma and disagrees with the poloidal angle's zero. They are now Z = Z_mid with R beyond or inside the magnetic axis, where Z_mid is the height the data's midplane_identifier selects, the magnetic-axis height by default.
  • Internal inductance (same node) now names its li(3) form, matching its equation.

Both reviews asked for a test pinning the new wording. That is declined: a test pinning approved text is a change detector, and the accessor tests already show the registry is what renders. The midplane fragment says a before-capture of the accessor is on disk. Only the after-capture exists; the before text was checked against the base blob instead.
