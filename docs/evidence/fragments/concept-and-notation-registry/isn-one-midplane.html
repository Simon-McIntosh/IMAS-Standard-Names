The outboard and inboard midplane loci carried a per-dataset height: the height the data's midplane identifier selected, and the magnetic-axis height when none was given. A name whose quantity is taken at a stated height therefore read two heights. Under the lead ruling in concept-and-notation-registry §10 the midplane is now defined once, by the major-radius extremum of the flux surface in question: maximum on the low-field side for the outboard midplane, minimum on the high-field side for the inboard midplane.

The equation \theta = \operatorname{atan2}(Z_{\mathrm{axis}} - Z,\; R - R_{\mathrm{axis}}) is unchanged; the added sentence states where the zero of that equation meets the redefined outboard midplane.
